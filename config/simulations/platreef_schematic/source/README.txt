PLATREEF MINE - SCHEMATIC UNDERGROUND MODEL (v2026-10-07)
Local mine grid, metres. X=east, Y=north, Z=elevation relative to Shaft 1 collar (negative = below surface). Origin = Shaft 1 collar. Not georeferenced.

FILES
platreef_layout.json  full graph (nodes, edges, stopes) + metadata, for simulators / wearable back-ends
nodes.csv edges.csv stopes.csv  same data as tables (GIS / spreadsheet / database import)
platreef_layout.dxf   AutoCAD R12 ASCII: 3D lines for drifts/shafts/raises (layers by level), 3D-face boxes for rooms and stopes, points for beacons
platreef_model.obj    mesh/lines for Blender, Unity, Unreal, MeshLab (Z-up)

ACCURACY
Shaft depths/diameters, level elevations (750/850/950/996 m), orebody depth range, dip-direction schematic and ore-flow concept come from public Ivanhoe releases. Positions of rooms, drifts, stopes, ore passes and Shaft 3 collar are inferred. Not survey data; not for engineering or safety decisions.
Drift length in this layout (m): {750: 985, 850: 1460, 950: 2241, 996: 221, 1000: 46, 'x': 44}

SOURCES
Ivanhoe Mines news releases 2017-2026 (shafts, levels, Shaft 2 raisebore, Shaft 3 conversion)
Mining Weekly / Engineering News / International Mining / Mining-Technology project articles
2017 DFS and 2022 FS summaries (orebody depth 700-1,200 m, T1+T2 zones up to ~29 m, avg ~24 m)
